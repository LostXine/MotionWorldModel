Motion World Models for Robot Control

Xiang Li Yichi Zhang E Ro Nguyen Kanchana Ranasinghe Ryan Burgert Michael S. Ryoo
Stony Brook University
This website was created on Feb 27, 2026

Abstract

Motion World Model (MWM) is a dual-system Vision–Language–Action (VLA) framework. It leverages a pretrained Image-Text-to-Video diffusion model as System 2 to jointly predict future RGB frames and dense pixel motion sequences. By explicitly modeling dense motion dynamics in addition to visual appearance, MWM provides richer predictive representations of future states. The action expert (System 1) substantially benefits from these motion-aware predictions, achieving improved performance compared to conventional World Models (WM) that predict RGB observations alone.
